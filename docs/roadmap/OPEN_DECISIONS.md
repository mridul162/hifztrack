# Open product and technical decisions

Open and conditional decisions below must not be treated as fully resolved. Recommendations remain recommendations until approved; approved decisions are retained for traceability. DEC-001 (source recovery) is resolved: both originals are preserved under `docs/source/`.

| ID | Question and why it matters | Options and consequences | Decision / recommendation | Approval / status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DEC-002 | Which canonical Quran dataset/version, script and page convention should be shipped? It determines stable references, pagination, licensing and import integrity. | Use an internally maintained validated dataset (source recommendation); use a licensed supplied dataset; defer displayed text and retain reference-only data. Choices affect license/coverage and maintenance. | **Conditionally approved:** retain Tanzil Quran Text Uthmani v1.1 verbatim as the Arabic text source, with the intended Madani 604-page convention; Surah/Ayah is canonical; translations are excluded initially; other Mushaf editions deferred. HifzTrack will maintain its own structured Quran metadata; the existing Tanzil metadata XML is not an application dependency. See ADR-014 for proposed metadata categories and evidence gates. | CONDITIONALLY APPROVED; verify Arabic provenance/notice before public release; establish source/usage conditions for each further metadata category and independently verify page boundaries before those mappings are integrated. |
| DEC-004 | What exact status transitions/verification rule governs Ayah progress? | Source names NOT_STARTED, LEARNING, MEMORIZED, STRONG and permits regression. | **Resolved / approved:** learner explicitly controls status per user/Ayah; transitions, including regression, are manual. STRONG requires explicit learner confirmation. Sabaq counts, mentor assessments, and revision do not change status automatically. See ADR-015. | RESOLVED / APPROVED 2026-11-23. |
| DEC-005 | How do repeated submissions, cancellation/correction, and status history interact? | Sabaq is historical activity separate from current progress; overlapping and repeated submissions must not inflate unique Ayah progress. | **Resolved / approved:** preserve every submission; cancel by changing SUBMITTED to CANCELLED without deleting history; correct by cancelling the incorrect record and creating a corrected one. Neither cancellation nor correction changes current status; only explicit status updates do. See ADR-016. | RESOLVED / APPROVED 2026-11-23. |
| DEC-006 | What exact default visibility should new memberships receive? | Source recommends private or summary-level defaults; private maximizes confidentiality, summary eases accountability but exposes aggregate information. Five source categories are overall progress, memorization logs, revision logs, goals, and assessments. | **Resolved / approved:** each new membership episode defaults to overall progress `SUMMARY`; memorization logs, revision logs, goals, and assessments default disabled. Each category is independently controlled. See ADR-018. | RESOLVED / APPROVED 2026-11-24. |
| DEC-007 | What happens to previously shared data and group-created artifacts after membership ends? | Revoke future reads only; remove member data from group artifacts; retain only anonymized aggregates. Each changes history, privacy, and group continuity. | Future access revocation is required; retention treatment must be approved. | Yes. |
| DEC-008 | Confirm role cardinality/assignment within one membership. | Source schema uses one ADMIN/MENTOR/MEMBER enum per membership; person may have different roles across groups and groups may have multiple admins/mentors. Multiple simultaneous roles per membership would require a different model. | Use one role per membership as the source baseline; confirm any need for additive roles before schema. | Confirm. |
| DEC-009 | Approve authentication and account identifier/session policy. | Source recommends email or phone identifier, Argon2id, secure HTTP-only cookies, CSRF protection, auth/invitation rate limits, and no long-lived JWT in localStorage. Recovery/MFA and provider choice remain unspecified. | Email identifier; Argon2id; PostgreSQL-backed server-side sessions; Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite cookies; server-side logout revocation. Recovery and MFA deferred. Owner approved signed double-submit CSRF token plus allowed-Origin validation, and PostgreSQL fixed-window registration/login limits (5 registration/IP, 10 login/IP+email, 30 login/IP per 15 minutes); generic 429 without account lockout. Invitation throttling remains for GROUP scope. | RESOLVED for AUTH-01 baseline and controls; invitation throttling remains for later GROUP design. |
| DEC-010 | What are reporting periods/timezone rules for daily/weekly/monthly calculations? | User-local timezone; group timezone; UTC boundaries. Results differ around day boundaries and affect goals/analytics. | Store timestamps consistently and define user-local periods before Release 2 calculations. | Yes. |
| DEC-011 | Should the product retain personal account-data export and extended CSV/PDF formats beyond original progress-report export? Also confirm boundary for institutional schema beyond optional organization-to-group association. | Original FR-17 progress-report export is P2/later; CSV/PDF and personal account export are additions. Source requires institutional data-model support but excludes complex institutional management from MVP. Extra portability/format scope adds privacy and implementation work. | Keep FR-17 at P2; keep personal account export/CSV/PDF as proposals until approved. Model optional organization relation, defer complex admin. | Yes for additions and schema detail. |
| DEC-012 | What language, Arabic/RTL, transliteration, accessibility, and device requirements are launch criteria? | Arabic-first, multilingual, or reference-only interface; accessibility conformance target and browser set vary effort. | Define before UX implementation, informed by intended audience and source docs. | Yes. |
| DEC-013 | What hosting, email, backups, observability, and CI services are approved? | Managed services vs self-hosted; choices affect cost and operational burden. | Keep Docker Compose as local baseline; select production providers separately. | Yes, before deployment. |
| DEC-014 | What are consent, retention, and data-processing rules for audio, WhatsApp, offline sync, AI, and exports? | Per-feature opt-in and deletion; provider processing; retention windows. Choices have privacy and operational consequences. | Each feature requires explicit opt-in, scoped access, and a documented deletion/retention policy before release. | Yes, before each feature. |
| DEC-015 | How much audit history and exceptional administrator access is required? | Log sensitive writes only; also log reads; privileged break-glass access with review. More coverage improves accountability but increases sensitive log data. | Audit sensitive role/sharing/assessment changes; define read logging and exception access with security policy. | Yes. |
| DEC-016 | Who may change a member's group-sharing settings? Source API PATCH path targets `/groups/{id}/members/{user_id}/sharing`, while privacy rules give the data owner control. | Member-only editing; group admin editing; member-edit plus admin-request/approval. Admin override can expose private progress; member-only may require a self-scoped route. | **Resolved / approved:** only the data owner can change their own settings for a group membership. Admin and mentor roles grant no override. Settings are independent per membership. See ADR-018. | RESOLVED / APPROVED 2026-11-24. |
| DEC-017 | How does membership rejoin/history work with unique `(group_id,user_id)` and status LEFT? | Re-activate same row (loses membership episodes unless events stored); create a new row (requires different uniqueness rule); retain row and add membership history. Each affects audit and settings retention. | Preserve membership history explicitly; decide uniqueness/lifecycle before schema. | Yes. |
| DEC-018 | Where is the boundary between P0 group dashboard statistics and P1 analytics? | P0 dashboard shows current-state/member summaries and group statistics; P1 FR-16 adds historical reports, participation analytics, and advanced metrics. Expanding P0 increases MVP scope; deferring all statistics would omit the P0 dashboard requirement. | Keep current-state and basic group summaries in P0; keep historical, participation, date-based reports and advanced metrics in P1. Exact basic summary measures still need definition. | Yes for the P0 summary metric set. |
| DEC-019 | Is an explicit assessment-triggered status-transition workflow ever permitted? | Default is a separate assessment record with no automatic change to learner Ayah status. An explicitly approved workflow could allow mentor proposal/confirmation or a defined assessment result to cause a status transition. Consequences include authority, audit, and transaction coupling. | Preserve separate assessment by default. Any future status change requires an explicit approved business rule and must be transactional with the assessment. | Yes before any coupling is implemented. |
| DEC-020 | Does group deletion mean hard delete, soft-deactivate, or archive? | Hard delete removes group history; soft-deactivate blocks new activity; archive preserves read-only records. Each affects privacy, audit and rejoin. | Do not hard-delete historical group records without a retention policy; choose lifecycle action before GROUP implementation. | Yes. |
| DEC-021 | What does “multi-tenant” mean for isolation when a global user belongs to groups across organizations? | Organization-level hard data isolation; group-scoped authorization with shared global identity; tenant per group. These choices affect identifiers, query scoping, analytics and whether one personal record can span organizations. | Preserve global user identity and group-scoped roles/sharing from source; avoid organization hard-isolation assumptions until owner defines tenant boundary. | Yes before institutional schema/API. |
| DEC-022 | Is revision analytics part of P0 revision tracking or P1 historical analytics? | Phase 4 roadmap mentions revision analytics, while FR-16 classifies historical reports/analytics as P1. | Owner approved for REV-01: defer basic summaries and detailed revision analytics; P0 stores and lists revision history only. Revisit only with explicit scope approval. | RESOLVED / APPROVED 2026-11-24. |
| DEC-023 | How should the product represent summary versus detailed sharing, particularly for overall progress? | Keep the five booleans and define combinations as summary/detail (simple schema, but combinations may be unclear); add `PRIVATE`/`SUMMARY`/`DETAILED` overall progress level (clear overall view, separate booleans still govern logs/goals/assessments); use levels per category (precise but more complex settings and schema). | **Resolved / approved:** overall progress uses `PRIVATE`, `SUMMARY`, or `DETAILED`; memorization logs, revision logs, goals, and assessments each have an independent Boolean control. The permitted fields and behavior are specified in the business rules and ADR-018. | RESOLVED / APPROVED 2026-11-24. |
| DEC-024 | How are group-goal types and completion calculations represented? | Source `user_goals` has `goal_type` (`NEW_MEMORIZATION`/`REVISION`), but candidate `group_goals` omits it although the feature scope includes both. Options: add matching `goal_type`; use a separate typed group-goal model; use a generic label/rule (less enforceable). Completion may aggregate member activity or track group activity independently, with different meanings. | Do not finalize schema; align group goal types with approved product semantics and explicitly define completion calculation. | Yes before Release 2 goal schema. |
